Something about this world feels off.

Probably, many people sense it.  
But saying so out loud won't move the world.  
So it seems wiser to accept the rules and try to live skillfully within them.

How many points you scored.  
How much you earned.  
How many "likes" you got.

With these yardsticks—treated almost as though they were truth—  
we've measured ourselves since childhood,  
and even now, people measure other people, label them, rank them,  
and quietly stick a price tag on them.

I'm embarrassed to say, I do this too.

And yet, I believe this:  
People can step away from these old yardsticks and live in ways that are far more truly their own.

There are people who don't care how others measure them.  
There are people who give real value to someone else, expecting nothing in return.  
There are people who think seriously about the future and act on it.  
I want to build a world where people like this are rewarded, as a matter of course.

Rather than a world where people jostle to outrank one another,  
I want to see a world where people look at one another and think,  
"Ah, the way that person lives is beautiful"—  
a world where people admire one another.

The yardsticks we use today were made by people.  
Which means people should be able to remake them.

If you, too, sense that something about this world is off—  
I hope you'll never forget that feeling.

And I hope that, together with Gaudiy, you'll help create a new yardstick, a new story:  
the "Fan State."

Yuya Ishikawa  
Founder, Gaudiy
