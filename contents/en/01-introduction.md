Something about this world is off.

Probably, many people have noticed.  
But speaking up doesn't move the world.  
So it seems wiser to accept the rules and try to live skillfully within them.

How many points did you score.  
How much did you earn.  
How many people thought "like."

With the very yardstick that has measured us  
since childhood, as if it were truth itself,  
people still measure other people, label them, rank them,  
and quietly stick a price tag on them.

Embarrassingly, I am no different.

And yet, I believe this:  
People can step away from that old yardstick and live far more truly as themselves.

There are people who don't care how others measure them.  
There are people who give real value to someone else, even with nothing expected in return.  
There are people who think seriously about the future and act on it.  
I want to build a world where people like that are naturally rewarded.

Not a world where people jockey for position over one another,  
but one where people look at each other and think,  
"Ah, the way that person lives is beautiful" —  
a world where people admire one another. That's the world I want to see.

The yardstick we use now was made by people.  
Which means people should be able to remake it.

If you, too, sense that something about this world is off,  
I hope you never forget that feeling.

And I hope that, together with Gaudiy, you'll help create a new yardstick, a new story —  
the **Fandom State**.

Gaudiy Founder  
Yuya Ishikawa
